Adaptive waypoint allocation has mature adjacent formulations, but “choose more knots where cuff force and motion variation coincide” remains a study-specific heuristic here. Formal hp error estimators concern numerical error, whereas this study’s refinement criterion concerns observed physical response. Those must not be equated.

Path and speed are often separated in robotics. The current polynomial certificate interface admits C2 quintic segments, not arbitrary nonlinear path-time compositions. Fixed geometric nodes plus redistributed Hermite segment times provide a useful experiment, but introduce small geometric changes between nodes. A later exact speed layer would need an independently specified representation and certificate, rather than borrowing a proof for different coefficients.

Curvature describes shape in a chosen coordinate plane. Velocity TV describes time evolution. Jerk measures changes in acceleration. None alone measures subjective comfort. The native data should decide whether any is a useful local diagnostic; correlation is not a physical force model.

The defensible scope of this package is a concrete combination: complete robot + cuff contact + passive Human dynamics, unchanged task and safety execution, internal continuous hip/knee coordination, full-task cumulative cuff-force objective, explicit applied-command ownership, and matched-parent perturbations. This is a reproducible experimental configuration, not evidence of uniqueness, patient generalization, host qualification or clinical benefit. Human-in-the-loop preference learning is related future work; no active human optimization or learning is performed here.
