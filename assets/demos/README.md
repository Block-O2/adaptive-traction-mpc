The homepage uses the existing
[Stage-4 MuJoCo GIF](../../stages/stage4_adaptive_control/results/media/readme/hero_fixed_vs_adaptive.gif).
It shows a UR10e surrogate assisting a modeled lower limb through a cuff,
with fixed and adaptive MPC panels. The media is a saved-state replay and is
retained in its original location.

No new rollout, training or physical experiment is needed to view it. The
[video/source manifest](../../stages/stage4_adaptive_control/results/summaries/professor_video_manifest.json)
records the replay pipeline and source traces. Existing Stage-4 replay tools
are described in the [stage guide](../../stages/stage4_adaptive_control/README.md).

Later Stage-5 material remains in the archive. The homepage GIF must not be
described as a demonstration of 18D, H150, learning integration or current
host-clock qualification. No videos or raw datasets are duplicated here.
