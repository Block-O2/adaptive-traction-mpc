## Objective

Recover a deployable, causal, control-sufficient adaptive MPC that executes a
broad family of physically feasible rehabilitation references under hidden
variation in effective Human dynamics, leg geometry and placement, cuff
attachment, initial configuration, and other material session conditions.
Perfect recovery of individual physical parameters is not required.

The V1 `BLOCKED_IDENTIFIABILITY` result is retained as
**individual-physical-parameter identifiability negative evidence**. In
particular, `L2` and cuff fraction `f` are not individually observable from a
cuff-only kinematic channel when only `sc=fL2` enters that channel. V2 may use
composite/effective parameters, uncertainty sets, belief/history state, safe
causal probing, or another defensible representation.

## Deployable boundary and truth firewall

Deployable estimation, model authority, candidate generation, MPC prediction,
and action selection may use only causal observations/history and explicitly
documented commissioning/calibration signals that could exist on the intended
system. Hidden simulator geometry, dynamics, placement, cuff location, initial
Human state, or task-generator truth may be used only to generate cases,
construct an explicitly labeled oracle, evaluate, or diagnose after execution.

A campaign-level identifiability block requires indistinguishable hidden setups
that need incompatible decisions or disjoint admissible controls, plus evidence
that no conservative common policy works. Individual parameter ambiguity is
not such a block.

## Task and comparison contract

- References must be physically feasible within the registered plant, robot,
  contact, ROM, and motion envelope.
- The benchmark may receive a reference trajectory and its velocity/profile.
- Task identity must not be hard-coded into the adaptive representation.
- Development and held-out cases must be generated by separate frozen rules.
- The final Phase-2 hidden domain must vary dynamics, relevant geometry,
  placement, cuff attachment, initial condition, and multiple reference
  trajectories continuously.
- Intended comparisons are oracle, recovered adaptive, wrong/fixed nominal,
  no-adaptation ablation, and the strongest reproducible historical adaptive
  baseline where it is meaningfully comparable.
- Shared comparisons retain the same task, cost meaning, constraints, horizon,
  solver family, and evaluation duration unless a difference is preregistered.

## Phase gates

### Phase 0 — functional historical recovery

PASS requires the strongest actual historical adaptive capability to be
reproduced or honestly bounded, the Stage-5 scope loss to be traced, all major
functional assumptions to be explicit, and independent Auditor approval.

### Phase 1 — control-sufficient adaptive MPC recovery

Development proceeds through hidden dynamics, cuff, placement, geometry,
combined setup, multiple reference profiles, and mechanically valid high-ROM.
Held-out cases are not used for tuning. Primary evidence is closed-loop task,
tracking, constraint, force/contact, solver, and recovery behavior; parameter
error is diagnostic only.

### Phase 2 — formal unknown-setup/unknown-task validation

Before viewing results, freeze continuous hidden ranges, task family,
development/held-out rules, metrics, gates, budget, exclusions, and seeds.
Preserve all failures. Adaptive performance must satisfy the preregistered
closed-loop gates, materially beat wrong/fixed nominal where hidden variation
matters, and document its gap to oracle.

### Phase 3 — modern Human-waypoint integration

After Phase 2 passes, integrate the adaptive/control-sufficient state into the
state-feedback Human-waypoint MPC. Production planning is event/state driven,
not a fixed-r template or an absolute 2.2/0.5/2.2 s clock. The candidate API
must expose a clean future value hook without implementing or training RL, and
must log observations, adaptive state, candidates, selected/executed waypoint,
next observation, local cost, completion, and provenance.

## High-ROM and mechanics

Investigate configurations near 120--130 degrees per Human joint only where
ROM, contact/table clearance, robot reachability, motion constraints, and model
validity permit. Report the validated joint-combination envelope and separate
plant/mechanical limits from controller limits. Constraints may not be disabled
or the domain shrunk post hoc to obtain a pass.

## Repair, audit, and stop rules

Builder and independent Auditor are separate roles. The Auditor challenges
truth leakage, finite-case memorization, task hard-coding, post-hoc domain
shrinking, dynamics absorbing geometry, unavailable exact initial state,
frozen/oracle geometry, deterministic copies, and unnecessary complexity.
There are at most four materially distinct repair cycles per major phase.

The only terminal labels are `PHASE_3_READY`,
`BLOCKED_CONTROL_SUFFICIENCY`, `BLOCKED_NEW_PHYSICAL_INFORMATION`,
`BLOCKED_CONTRACT_CHANGE_REQUIRED`, `BLOCKED_METHOD_EXHAUSTION`, and
`BLOCKED_TOOL_OR_QUOTA`, with the evidentiary meanings in the user contract.
