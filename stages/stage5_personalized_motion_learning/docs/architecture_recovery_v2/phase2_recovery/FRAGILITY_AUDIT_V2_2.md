# V2.2 Fragility Audit

Status: **ACCEPTED WITH MATERIAL FORMAL-RISK WARNING**

- Aggregate development improvement over V2.1 is only 1.86% median and 1.15%
  p95; formal reversal is plausible.
- Standard-ROM median improved 0.98%, while high-ROM median worsened 0.65%.
- Coordinated/high-ROM and two-rate/standard cells had positive mean paired
  deltas; the benefit is not uniform by profile/ROM cell.
- Candidate moment peak rose from 31.42 Nm to 33.66 Nm but remained well below
  the unchanged 60 Nm gate. Force peak was unchanged at 144.59 N.
- Maximum coefficient total variation reached 46.19 Nm and maximum residual
  sign reversals reached 19 in one case. These are reported adaptation-activity
  diagnostics, not evidence of anatomical convergence.
- Residual peak 8.943 Nm and coefficient-row norm peak 6.683 Nm remained below
  12 Nm. There were zero coefficient projections and zero observed,
  prediction, chosen-rollout, or legacy cap hits.
- Commissioning-only failed 1/24, so the 33.42% median advantage is accompanied
  by a completion advantage but must still be retested under fresh formal
  cases.

The preregistered development rule permits freeze. These fragilities prohibit
claiming Phase-2 capability or entering Phase 3 until a fresh formal retry
passes every unchanged gate.
