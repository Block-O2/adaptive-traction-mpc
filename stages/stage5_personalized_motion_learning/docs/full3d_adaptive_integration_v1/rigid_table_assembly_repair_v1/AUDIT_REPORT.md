## Separate v2 geometry-envelope supplement

The v2 preparation reads v1 **assembly diagnostics**, not controller outcomes, and its case mapping preserves all 24 v1 case hashes. I compared every v1/v2 case JSON: only `balanced_near_upper_current_rom_r02` changes, solely in hidden hip-z, by +1.873398 mm to +3.650576 mm. The added shift is `0.100000 − (−1.773398) = 1.873398 mm`, using the v1 initial sleeve signed gap and registered static clearance; the independently repeated assembly screen gives +0.100000 mm initial sleeve gap and 23/24 v2 initial/task-endpoint-valid cases. For `balanced_ordinary_r02`, the same vertical rule would require +7.178067 mm hip-z, beyond the frozen +6 mm upper bound, so it remains invalid. This establishes only that no legal placement was found **by this vertical rule**, not that every possible assembly is infeasible.

The one newly admitted v2 case was run separately with the same `run_one` controller flags and ended `ABORTED: ACTIVE_RECOVERY_NO_FEASIBLE_SEGMENT`; recovery did not succeed and task duration/decisions were zero. I recomputed its 28,080 contiguous 0.25 ms contact intervals: thigh–bed count, peak and impulse are zero; shank–bed peak is 129.565 N and impulse 124.444 N·s. Independently repositioning the saved Human and CR12 states at all 1,406 recorded 5 ms trace boundaries gives minimum sleeve–bed signed distance **−3.132480 mm at t=5.98 s**, agreeing with the v2 geometry artifact. The trace labels this boundary `COMMISSIONING`, while the summary records that active recovery was entered. The sleeve mask remains collision-disabled, so zero thigh contact cannot be generalized to a nonpenetrating full assembly. This is development evidence on a consumed case, not fresh qualification, continuous-time clearance proof, or an improvement to the v1 5/22 task-completion result.

## Limits and unresolved issues

`ASSEMBLY_VALID` means the **initial and task-endpoint** screen passed. It does not certify every commissioning reference, intermediate robot/cuff configuration or physical substep. The executed geometry reconstruction samples only 5 ms boundaries; the bed contact monitor covers thigh and shank at every 0.25 ms interval, not sleeve, cuff bar or robot signed distances at every interval. Base and moving link1 are inventoried at reset but excluded from the reported links 2–6 executed-path minimum. Disabled collision masks mean no reported sleeve/robot contact force is not proof of geometric separation. The cuff/sleeve shapes remain provisional model surrogates, not measured hardware envelopes.

The commissioning reference reaches geometrically impossible shank positions on the fixed hard table in every executed case. The real simulated Human instead contacts the table and deviates from that reference. The historical contract explicitly allowed and recorded commissioning shank contact, so this observation does not retroactively fail old formal cases. It limits interpretation of identification and tracking and has not been shown to cause each later abort. The −0.479 mm sleeve overlap additionally prevents a whole-assembly nonpenetration claim even for all 22 admitted setups.

The two initial sleeve-invalid cases remain unresolved **in v1**. The v2 supplement makes one initial/task-endpoint-valid but reveals a larger sampled sleeve overlap during execution and an active-recovery abort; the other remains initial-invalid. The 17 v1 task aborts and eight stale-plan events remain failures; the v1 5/22 completion count is not reliable full-domain recovery. The old cases were consumed for development, and neither the v1 nor v2 revised cases are fresh held-out evidence. Controlled planning-delay replay is not asynchronous hardware-real-time validation. The 0.1 mm installation gap is not a measured mounting tolerance or safety margin.

The v1 `FINAL_REPORT.md` correctly labels cuff peaks as recorded 5 ms boundary values, its cuff integral as 5 ms control-interval cost, its robot geometry minima as links 2–6, and its 22 runs as finalized executed-session artifacts. It keeps the two v1 sleeve-invalid cases explicit, separates the 22 admitted v1 setups from full path validity, and limits causal claims. `COMMANDS.md` lists the preparation, prefix, representative/broad runs, summaries, geometry audits, verifier and focused tests. The local dirty/untracked dependency basis still prevents a clean-clone reproduction claim. No historical artifact was rewritten by this audit.

Exactly one recommended next control-development objective: version and test a rigid-table-feasible, feedback-observed commissioning reference for the admitted v1 setups while holding estimator, task controller and physical limits fixed. The v2 supplement does not resolve the whole-path sleeve overlap or the one still initial-invalid setup; both require a separate later assembly/geometry decision before any full-24 qualification claim.
