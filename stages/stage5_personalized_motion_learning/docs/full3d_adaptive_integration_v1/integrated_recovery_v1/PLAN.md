# Integrated recovery plan

1. Snapshot exact local baseline/dependencies and preserve prior evidence.
2. Diagnose bed-thigh force extraction, geometry, contact Jacobian, penetration,
   impulse, initial conditions and mechanical consistency at matched states
   plus non-anomalous contrasts. Independent causal/design audit.
3. If mechanically credible, separate natural state-evolution output change
   from model/context-switch change; compare proposal/accepted/applied models
   on causal data. Select smallest coherent repair and document before outcomes.
4. Targeted physical sessions, followed by consumed old-24 adaptive development
   replay and new development conditions once coherent. Preserve all failures.
5. Freeze a stable candidate, independent freeze audit, then fresh paired
   current-domain qualification. Otherwise record the substantive blocker.
6. Final evidence audit, review package, exact status and one next step.

No old phase-letter boundary is a stopping rule. A material unexplained physical
model condition is a qualification blocker, not an invitation to tune physics.
