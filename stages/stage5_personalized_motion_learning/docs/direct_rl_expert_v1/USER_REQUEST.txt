8. Environment correctness gates
==================================================

Before training, verify:

A. known baseline/replay behavior can be reproduced through the environment

E. task COMPLETE requires real OUTBOUND/HOLD/RETURN

I. same seed/reset is deterministic where expected

Save:

Do not train main SAC until these gates PASS.

==================================================
9. Reward v1
==================================================

The scientific objective is still J_F_task.

Training reward may include structured task shaping.

Construct reward from:

- negative physical cuff force integral over the RL step
- task/progress potential shaping
- explicit valid task-completion reward if needed
- clear failure penalty

Do NOT reward:
- “large action”
- “hip leading”
- deviation from baseline
- similarity to CEM winner

The policy must be free to discover its own coordination.

Run explicit reward-hacking tests:

- do nothing
- abort early
- stall before HOLD
- skip RETURN
- exploit timeout
- cause safety rejection repeatedly
- shift load to moment

None may outperform a successful complete task under the training return in an unintended way.

Keep moment metrics independent.
Do not hide moment increases using an arbitrary reward weight.
