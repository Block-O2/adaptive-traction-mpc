# Superseded lifecycle artifact

This artifact is retained as the final audit of the former multi-candidate L4
lifecycle. Its per-candidate statistical evidence remains historical evidence,
but the lifecycle allowed many simultaneous pending candidates and terminated
them when a different candidate changed the incumbent reference.

The replacement non-default prototype and replay are in
`stage4_single_challenger_trust_audit_20260827`. They use exactly one fixed-
incumbent challenger at a time and an independently derived anytime alpha-
spending rule. No production controller or estimator behavior was changed.
