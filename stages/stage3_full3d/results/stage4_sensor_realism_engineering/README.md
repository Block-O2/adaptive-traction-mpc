# Stage 4 sensor-realism engineering artifacts

The five top-level JSON/NPZ pairs are the final deterministic engineering
cases. `suite_summary.json` and `summary.md` provide the compact comparison.
The `diagnostic_*` directories retain superseded preprocessing/boundary runs
for reproducibility; they are not additional registered cases.

These outputs are engineering evidence, not authoritative clinical or CR12
sensor validation. See
`../../docs/STAGE4_SENSOR_REALISM_ENGINEERING.md` for settings, interpretation,
and the exact reproduction command.
